## Playlist character

This is intentionally much more **North Indian cassette / bus / truck / auto** than a generic “best Bollywood songs” list.

The core sound is:
- **Aashiqui / Saajan / Sadak / Dil / Deewana**
- Kumar Sanu + Alka Yagnik + Udit Narayan
- Nadeem–Shravan-style romantic tracks
- Big T-Series-era cassette hits
- A few loud, instantly recognisable road songs such as *Tu Cheez Badi Hai Mast Mast* and *Saat Samundar Paar*

The later additions are kept because they fit the same listening culture even though several are from 2001–2003. For instance, *Dil Laga Liya Maine* is from *Dil Hai Tumhaara* (2002), *Dekhne Walon Ne* is from *Chori Chori Chupke Chupke* (2001), and *Jo Bhi Kasmein* is from *Raaz* (2002). citeturn0search2turn0search0turn0youtube107

*Love Tujhe Love Main* is from the 1995 *Barsaat* soundtrack, which is particularly on-brand for this playlist's Kumar Sanu / Nadeem–Shravan direction. citeturn0search5
